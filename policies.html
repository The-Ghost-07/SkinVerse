How downloads work

Digital items (wallpapers, cursors, themes, Rainmeter skins and desktop bundles) are delivered as files. After you check out, a download button appears for each item on the cart page, and again on that item's own page. Some items do not have their files uploaded yet and show "Download file coming soon".

Installing a desktop bundle (.rmskin)

  1. Install Rainmeter (free) from rainmeter.net. Bundles need Rainmeter 4.5 or newer on Windows 10 or 11.
  2. Download the .rmskin file from your order.
  3. Double-click the file and press Install in the window that opens.
  4. Follow the setup guide that comes with the bundle to set your screen size and load the layout.

Delivery

Digital items are available to download immediately after checkout, with no shipping fee. Desk items are physical products; shipping is not part of this demo, so the cart only records them.

Refunds

Because digital files can be copied once downloaded, digital items are generally not refundable. If a file is broken or does not match its description, contact us and we will fix it or replace it. Policies for physical desk items would be set before real sales begin.

Privacy

This site has no server database. Your cart, wishlist and purchases are kept only in your own browser (local storage) and are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them. The newsletter box is a demo and does not store or send your email.
